# Milk, sheep milk, whey, rennet and cider vinegar: 26.2 liquid rendering
- The common `LiquidSurfaceVisuals` renderer is used by all Barrel woods and the generic cauldron, preserving levels and animation.
- Cow milk has a new pale-white 16-frame sprite (`milk_still.png`, 16×256).
- Sheep milk has a distinctly warm cream 16-frame sprite (`sheep_milk_still.png`, 16×256).
- Rennet has a distinct beige 16-frame sprite (`rennet_still.png`, 16×256).
- Whey reuses the previously bundled animated `fluids/whey_still.png` and its Rustic-like .mcmeta (no empty static placeholder).
- Apple cider vinegar reuses the previously bundled animated `fluids/booze/cider_still.png` and its .mcmeta.
- All five are explicitly included in the Minecraft blocks atlas; sprite animation is automatically advanced by the standard atlas tick.
- Sheep milk and whey bottles now use **new actual 16×16 PNG assets** composited from vanilla 26.2 potion glass and tint overlay, preserving the binary alpha 0/255 project rule.
- No mechanics, recipes, item IDs, or inventories are changed in this visual-only patch.
- In-game verification: put 1 bucket each of milk, sheep milk, whey, rennet and vinegar into individual Barrels and cauldrons. Ensure all five show moving fluid, no missing texture, and distinct milk tones; remove each with matching bucket/bottle.
