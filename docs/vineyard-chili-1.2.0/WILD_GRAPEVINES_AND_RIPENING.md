# Wild grapevines and grape maturation — 1.2.0

Based on Farmer's Delight Refabricated 26.3 `WildCropBlock.java` and generated `wild_tomatoes.json`: wild crops are **broken** to obtain 1 seed (plus Fortune) and 20% chance of fruit. With a tool supporting the `shears_harvest` ability, the whole wild crop drops instead. Bone meal for the tall wild grapevines follows the FDR wild rice mechanism (occasional wild plant item); wild harvesting by right-click is not allowed.

Wild vines now use `WildGrapeBlock extends DoublePlantBlock`: red and white plants are each two blocks tall, following FDR wild rice for two-half placement and FDR wild tomato/rice loot. They appear in sparse, small patches in temperate biomes; see `WILD_GRAPES_SPAWN.md` for current frequencies and terrain rules. The four original sprites have been replaced by the approved irregular-bunch artwork. Existing `wild_chili` loot now likewise supports shears and FDR tomato seeds/Fortune/20% fruit. The obsolete `wild_grapes.png` is removed.

Cultivated `GrapeLeavesBlock` keeps the original `GRAPES` boolean and block IDs. Old `grapes=true` remains ripe, visible and harvestable. A new `FRUIT_STAGE` (0–3) adds tiny green, enlarged yellow-green and nearly ripe intermediate fruit (different palettes for red/white cultivars). Random ticks and bone meal move forward one stage at a time. The fourth growth event sets GRAPES true and reveals the existing approved ripe sprite. Only ripe grapes can be picked. Harvest resets both states. An occupied block below the hanging canopy clears unfinished or ripe fruit. No difference in wild-vs-cultivated fruit yield has been invented.

Test: `/give @p italiansdelight:wild_grape_vine`, `/give @p italiansdelight:wild_white_grape_vine`, and `/give @p italiansdelight:wild_chili`. Compare hand-breaking and shears on all wild plants, bone meal propagation, four-step growth on both cultivar canopies, ripe-only picking and an existing saved ripe vineyard. Wild patches appear only in newly generated chunks. Their two-block placement is checked with `/place feature` in the dedicated-server smoke test.
